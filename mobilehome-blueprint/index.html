The MobileHome Blueprint — Where the Margin Actually Lives
LAND

Cheap Dirt With Good Bones

Rural residential and agricultural parcels with manufactured-home use allowed by right are the base of the play. Watch flood zone, road access, and back-tax rollback risk. Bad zoning erases the deal before you even close.

HOME

New HUD-Code Beats Used

New HUD-code homes qualify for GSE financing (Fannie MH Advantage, Freddie CHOICEHome) once sited on a permanent foundation. Used homes rarely do. Financing exit is the difference between a retail buyer and a fire-sale chattel buyer.

SITE & UTILITIES

Septic Is The Silent Killer

Perc test before you close. Conventional septic runs $8-$12K; aerobic (ATU) can run $14-$18K in unfriendly soils; a sewer tap is often the cheapest option when it's available. Get quotes in writing before the land deposit is at risk.

EXIT

Chattel → Real Is Where The Uplift Lives

A permanent HUD-compliant foundation plus an engineer's certification plus retiring the chattel title creates a real-property parcel. The appraised value jumps 15-25%, financing options open up, and the buyer pool expands from cash-and-chattel to conventional FHA and GSE.

The Honest Read

MobileHome Blueprint deals live or die on three lines: land price with clean zoning, the septic and utility trifecta, and the chattel-to-real conversion. Miss any one and the retail exit compresses back to chattel comps — and the whole spread collapses. Use the 60-90 day price test rigorously; underpriced holds cost more in carry and stress than a small, disciplined price cut.

Sources: HUD Manufactured Home Program (Title VI), Fannie Mae MH Advantage guidelines, Freddie Mac CHOICEHome, U.S. Census Bureau Manufactured Housing Survey, MHI (Manufactured Housing Institute) annual reports.
